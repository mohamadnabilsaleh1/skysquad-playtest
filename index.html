How to play

  • High band: enemy fighters stream in and dive at you. Shoot them before they reach you. Each hit costs one plane.
  • Low band: loot crates. Break the front crate to grow your squad.
  • Every 100 fighters a boss parks on the red line and fires back.
  • Coins persist between attempts. Spend them in the lobby on fire rate, damage and coin revenue, then retry.
